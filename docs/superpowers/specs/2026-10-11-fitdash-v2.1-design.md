# fitdash v2.1 — design

Date: 2026-10-11
Status: approved in chat ("ok" to all five themes and items 1–5)
Builds on: `2026-10-11-fitdash-v2-design.md`. Everything there stays unless changed here.

## Goal

Make fitdash look and feel premium in Ultrahuman's visual language, let the user pick a day and a theme, and add the basics that were missing: Settings & About with a prominent Buy-me-a-coffee, deltas vs yesterday, naps, Week/Month trends, an estimated resting heart rate, and sharing the day as an image.

Visual references: `.superpowers/brainstorm/*/content/premium-uh.html` (Today, Sleep) and `themes.html` (five themes + day picker), and Mayur's Ultrahuman screenshots.

## Visual language

- **Type:** Inter (bundled: ExtraLight, Light, Regular, Medium) for text and numbers; JetBrains Mono (Regular, Medium) for labels, units, status words, nav labels and dates, always UPPERCASE with 0.08em tracking. Big numbers: Inter Light/ExtraLight, tracking −0.03em. Units sit after numbers in small mono caps ("9 HRS 6 MINS").
- **Status words** in mono caps and colour: OPTIMAL / GOOD (theme good colour), FAIR (warn), NEEDS ATTENTION (bad). Used on tiles and contributor rows.
- **Surfaces:** themed background gradient; cards are a faint fill with a 1 px hairline border, radius 18 dp; no heavy grey boxes.
- **Icons:** line icons only (1.7 px stroke style used by the nav). No emoji anywhere.
- **Orb:** textured "living" orb: several soft colour blobs inside the morphing shape, slowly rotating, with inner shading and an outer glow; colours come from the theme. Calibrating / no data uses a desaturated version.
- **Nav bar:** frosted pill, icon + mono-caps label per item.

## Themes

Five themes, chosen in Settings, default Midnight Teal. Each theme defines: background gradient, text, muted text, accent, good / warn / bad, card fill, hairline, chip fill, nav background, three orb colours, glow, on-orb text colour, whether the system bars use dark icons, and two style switches (grid background, monospace everywhere).

| Theme | Character |
|---|---|
| Midnight Teal | teal-tinted near-black, teal accent (Ultrahuman-like) |
| Pastel | light cream → lilac → mint, lavender accent, dark text, dark system-bar icons |
| Techno | pure black, faint cyan grid, neon cyan / magenta, monospace for all text |
| Graphite | black and white only, red only for "bad" |
| Sunrise | warm dark brown, amber / coral |

Score colours (rings, gauges, bars) interpolate the theme's bad → warn → good instead of the fixed traffic-light colours.

Exact colour values: the `.teal`, `.pastel`, `.techno`, `.graphite`, `.sunrise` CSS blocks in `themes.html`.

## Day picker

- Top bar on every page: ≡ (Settings) · `‹ SUN · 11 OCT ›` · ⓘ.
- Under it on Today: a 7-day strip (Mon–Sun of the selected week); each day shows a dot when it has sleep or steps data; tapping selects it.
- Tapping the date opens a calendar sheet (Material 3 date picker) limited to the 30-day window Health Connect gives us. `›` is disabled on today.
- The selected date drives all four pages. "Do this today" suggestions only show when the selected date is today.

## Today page

1. Top bar and week strip.
2. Orb with the Recovery score (or "—" + CALIBRATING / NO DATA), "RECOVERY ›".
3. Three stats in a row: SLEEP · RECOVERY · MOVEMENT, big light numbers with ▲ (good colour) / ▼ (bad colour) vs the previous day, nothing when either day is missing. Tapping Sleep or Movement swipes to that page.
4. Day list: Sleep row (duration · bed–wake times), one row per nap (duration · times), Steps row (steps of goal · %), each with a line icon and ›.
5. Contributors (when HRV or resting HR exist): rows with value, range line and a status word.
6. Suggestions (today only), as rows with line icons.
7. Two pill buttons: SHARE DAY · ✦ ASK AI.

## Sleep page

Semicircle gauge with the sleep score and a status tag; bed→wake span bar with times; a 2×2 tile grid (Total sleep, Time in bed, Restorative % = deep+REM share, Overnight HR = lowest 30-min average during the night, each with a status word); stages chart; Sleep architecture rows (Deep / Light / REM / Awake: duration · %, a bar for your share and a dashed bracket for the typical range: deep 13–23 %, light 45–60 %, REM 20–25 %, awake 0–5 %); overnight vitals when present; 7 nights.

## Activity page

Same language: step gauge with goal, hourly bars, tiles (Active minutes, Distance, Calories) with status words, 7 days.

## Trends page

A WEEK / MONTH segmented toggle (7 or 30 days) drives every chart: Recovery, Sleep score, Steps, Resting HR (recorded or estimated), HRV when present, then the other data types.

## Estimated resting heart rate

Fitbit writes raw heart-rate samples but no resting HR. When a day has no resting-HR record, fitdash estimates it: the lowest 30-minute rolling average of heart-rate samples inside that night's main sleep; if there is no sleep, the 10th percentile of that day's samples (needs at least 30 samples). Estimated values are used for readiness and shown with an "EST." tag. Recorded values always win.

## Settings & About (≡)

A full-screen sheet, sections:
- **Support fitdash**: a prominent card at the top, "Buy me a coffee" → `https://buymeacoffee.com/thepsygeek` (shown when `SHOW_SUPPORT`).
- **Theme**: five swatches.
- **Goals**: daily steps (default 9,000, steps of 500), sleep hours (default 8, steps of 0.5).
- **Units**: km / miles.
- **Data**: Data sources (the existing list), "Open Health Connect".
- **About**: why fitdash exists ("Google Fit and Health Connect hold your data but show it plainly. fitdash is a calm, private dashboard on top of them: no account, no cloud, no internet access."), open source under GPL-3.0 with a GitHub link (`https://github.com/mayurrawte/fitdash`), a "View on GitHub" link, version.

Settings persist in `SharedPreferences`. Goals feed the metrics (step goal, sleep target) instead of the fixed constants.

The ⓘ button keeps opening the short Data-sources sheet.

## Share day

SHARE DAY renders a 1080×1350 image of the day (orb with score, the three stats, sleep and steps rows, date, small "fitdash" wordmark) in the current theme and opens the Android share sheet with it (via a FileProvider in the app cache).

## Out of scope

Widget, notifications, MCP server.

## Testing

Unit tests: estimated resting HR (sleep window, fallback percentile, minimum samples, recorded wins), deltas vs previous day, naps listing, selected-date build (past date: no suggestions, activity for that date), goals feeding metrics, 30-day trend series, status words. On device: every page in every theme, day picker, calendar, Settings, share image, at 1.3× font scale.
