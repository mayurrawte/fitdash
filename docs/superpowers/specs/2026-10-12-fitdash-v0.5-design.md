# fitdash v0.5 — design: complete the data

Date: 2026-10-12
Status: draft, from the 2026-10-12 review (gaps versus Google Fit, UI fine-tuning)
Builds on: `2026-10-11-fitdash-v2.1-design.md`. Everything there stays unless changed here.

## Goal

Show everything Health Connect already gives us that a Google Fit or Fitbit user expects and fitdash currently drops: workouts, heart rate through the day, history older than 30 days, full blood pressure, sleep efficiency and awakenings. Fix the comparisons that mislead on a partial day. Raise the type floor and touch targets so the app reads as premium on a real phone, not only in mockups.

v0.6 (the Ultrahuman layer: contributors for every score, sleep debt, day timeline, action-first home, modules) and v0.7 (widget, journal tags, weekly review, cycle) are separate specs.

## Decisions

| Topic | Decision |
|---|---|
| Workouts | Read full exercise sessions (type, title) and aggregate distance, calories, steps, average and max heart rate over each session. Rows on Today and Activity, a detail sheet per workout. |
| Heart rate | A heart-rate card on Today with the day's line, min, max and latest. A Heart rate metric detail sheet. Heart rate leaves "Other data". |
| History | Declare and request `READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY`. When granted, the day picker and Trends reach back 180 days; Trends and detail sheets gain a 6-month view of weekly averages. Baselines stay 30 days. Raw heart-rate samples stay 30 days. |
| Blood pressure | One "Blood pressure" tile showing systolic/diastolic. Detail sheet plots both. |
| Sleep | Efficiency and awakenings as tiles and metrics. Latency computed and shown in the Total sleep detail sheet. |
| Partial day | On today, the Movement delta compares with yesterday at the same hour. Distance and Calories tiles show no status word on today, and their 7-day average excludes the selected day. |
| Type floor | No text smaller than 11 sp. Nav labels 10 sp. |
| Touch targets | Every tappable control has a 48 dp touch area. |
| Glyph icons | No text glyphs as icons. ▲ ▼ › ▾ ✓ ☾ ☀ ✦ become drawn or vector icons. |
| Reload | Resuming the app within 2 minutes of the last successful read does not re-read Health Connect. Pull to refresh always reads. |
| Active minutes goal | A setting (default 30, step 5, 10–120). |
| Recovery shown twice | Unchanged in v0.5. The trio is reworked with the contributors model in v0.6. |

## Data layer

### Workouts

`HealthReader` reads `ExerciseSessionRecord` in full and, for each session, runs one `aggregate` over the session's time range for `DistanceRecord.DISTANCE_TOTAL`, `ActiveCaloriesBurnedRecord.ACTIVE_CALORIES_TOTAL`, `StepsRecord.COUNT_TOTAL`, `HeartRateRecord.BPM_AVG` and `HeartRateRecord.BPM_MAX`, each only when its read permission is granted. A session shorter than 2 minutes is ignored.

```kotlin
data class Workout(
    val start: Instant, val end: Instant,
    val type: Int,              // ExerciseSessionRecord.EXERCISE_TYPE_*
    val title: String?,
    val distanceMeters: Double?, val kcal: Double?, val steps: Long?,
    val avgHr: Double?, val maxHr: Double?,
    val app: String,            // dataOrigin package, for "via Fitbit"
)
```

`HealthSnapshot.exercise: List<Interval>` becomes `workouts: List<Workout>`. Active minutes is the union of workout spans (overlaps merged) on the selected day.

Type names come from one table in `ui/Copy.kt` (`workoutName(type)`): Running, Walking, Hiking, Cycling, Swimming, Strength, HIIT, Yoga, Pilates, Elliptical, Rowing, Stair climbing, Dancing, Football, Basketball, Tennis, Badminton, Boxing, Martial arts, Skiing, Snowboarding, Skating, Golf, Calisthenics, Stretching, Wheelchair, Other. Unmapped constants show "Workout". When `title` is set and is not just the type name, it is shown instead of the type name.

Icons: six new line icons in `NavIcons` (run, bike, swim, strength, yoga, generic flame) chosen by a `workoutIcon(type)` table; everything else uses the generic icon.

### Heart rate through the day

Raw samples are already read. New metrics in `metrics/Heart.kt`:

```kotlin
data class HeartDay(val min: Int, val max: Int, val avg: Int, val latest: Sample, val line: List<Sample?>)
fun heartDay(snapshot: HealthSnapshot, date: LocalDate, zone: ZoneId): HeartDay?
```

`line` is the day's samples bucketed to 5-minute means from 00:00 to 24:00 (288 slots). A slot with no sample is `null`, and a run of more than 6 null slots (30 minutes) breaks the drawn line. `HeartDay` is `null` when the day has fewer than 10 samples.

A new `MetricKind.HEART_RATE` whose daily series is the day's mean. "Heart rate" is removed from `GENERIC_TYPES` (it stays in `DESIGNED_TYPES` for the Data sources list).

### History

The manifest declares `android.permission.health.READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY`. `allPermissions` includes it, so the first grant screen asks for it. If it is denied, everything behaves as today.

When granted, `HealthReader.read` uses two windows:

- **Long window, 180 days:** sleep sessions, resting HR, HRV, SpO₂, respiratory rate, skin temperature, weight, height, body fat, blood pressure, blood glucose, VO₂ max, and the daily aggregates (steps, distance, calories, active calories, floors, elevation, hydration, nutrition). These are small.
- **Short window, 30 days:** raw heart rate, hourly steps, power, speed. These are large and only matter for the recent days.

`Tuning.BASELINE_DAYS` stays 30 and all baselines, ranges and the calibration rule are unchanged. A new `Tuning.HISTORY_DAYS = 180`. `DAYS_BACK` becomes a value on `TodayData` (`29` or `179`) rather than a constant, and the week strip, top bar and date picker use it.

`TodayData.trend` grows to the available window. Trends and metric detail sheets get a third segment, **6M**, which plots weekly averages (Monday-start weeks, up to 26 points) with a month label under the first week of each month. Week and Month are unchanged. Hourly steps and the heart-rate card are only available for the last 30 days; on an older date those cards are hidden.

Settings → Data gains a row **"Read older data"** when the permission is declared but not granted. Tapping it launches the permission request.

### Blood pressure

`GenericType` gains an optional `second` extractor. Blood pressure extracts systolic as the value and diastolic as the second. The tile shows `121/79 mmHg` (latest reading of the selected day, or the latest overall if the day has none, with the date in the note). The detail sheet draws two lines (systolic in accent, diastolic in muted) with a legend, and the MIN · AVG · MAX row is for systolic.

### Sleep

`SleepSummary` gains:

- `efficiency: Double` = asleep ÷ time in bed, 0–1.
- `awakenings: Int` = number of AWAKE stages that start after the first non-awake stage and end before the last non-awake stage. Nights without stages: `null`.
- `latency: Duration?` = session start to the first non-awake stage. Nights without stages: `null`.

New `MetricKind.EFFICIENCY` (unit %) and `MetricKind.AWAKENINGS` (unit "", integer).

Status words: efficiency ≥ 90 % OPTIMAL, ≥ 85 % GOOD, ≥ 75 % FAIR, else NEEDS ATTENTION. Awakenings ≤ 2 OPTIMAL, ≤ 4 GOOD, ≤ 6 FAIR, else NEEDS ATTENTION.

### Small corrections

- `dailyActivity` keeps a day when it has steps, distance **or** calories.
- Hydration is removed from "Other data" (the Water card already shows it).
- `TodayViewModel.select` compares against `clock()` for "is today", and `load()` refreshes `lastNow` so the app does not show "That day" for today after midnight.
- `load()` on resume is skipped when the last successful read is under 2 minutes old; `onRefresh` passes `force = true`.

## Pages

### Today

Order after the trio: Today heading · Sleep row · nap rows · Steps row · **one row per workout** · Water card · **Heart rate card** · Contributors · Do this today · pills.

- **Workout row:** icon by type; title "Running" (or the session title); subtitle "32m · 5.1 km · 7:12 AM" (distance only when present; calories instead when distance is absent). Tap opens the workout sheet.
- **Heart rate card** (`SectionCard("HEART RATE")`, trailing "latest 72 bpm"): a 64 dp line chart of the day's 5-minute means, the night's sleep span shaded in the deep colour at 12 % alpha, each workout span shaded in the accent at 12 % alpha, a dashed baseline at the day's resting HR when known. Under it a row: MIN · AVG · MAX. On a past day "latest" becomes the day's last reading. Hidden when `heartDay` is null. Tap opens the Heart rate detail sheet.
- **Movement delta on today:** `previous.movement` is yesterday's steps up to the current hour, as a percentage of the goal. On a past day it stays the full previous day.

### Sleep

The tile grid becomes six tiles in this order: Total sleep, Time in bed, Efficiency, Restorative, Awakenings, Overnight HR. Efficiency and Awakenings tiles are hidden on a night without stages (grid falls back to four). The Total sleep detail sheet shows "Fell asleep in 14m" under the explanation when latency is known.

### Activity

- Steps gauge keeps its layout but the arc gradient is accent → good (the deep → REM gradient stays on Sleep only). `SemiGauge` takes `from`/`to` colours.
- **Workouts section** after the tiles: `SectionLabel("WORKOUTS")`, a `ListRow` per workout (same row as Today), then a muted line "3 this week · 1h 42m". Hidden when the day has no workouts and the week has none.
- Active tile: goal from settings. Note reads "GOAL 30 MIN DONE" or "12 MIN TO GO".
- Distance and Calories tiles: on today, no status word; note "7-DAY AVG 6.2 KM". On a past day status as now, with the average over the seven days ending the day before.

### Trends

- Segments: WEEK · MONTH · **6M** (6M only when history is granted; otherwise two segments as now).
- Month and 6M bar charts get axis labels: Month shows the day of month under every 7th bar starting from the first; 6M shows the month name under the first week of each month.
- Line charts keep gaps: a missing day is a break in the line, not a collapsed point. Right edge shows the min and max values in tiny mono.
- BMI tile moves into a **Body** group with Weight (sparkline of daily weight, latest value, unit kg or lb by the units setting) and Body fat when present. "Other data" keeps what remains, grouped under **Heart** (blood pressure, VO₂ max, blood glucose), **Body** and **Other**.

### Workout sheet

`ModalBottomSheet`, same chrome as the metric detail sheet: type name (or title), "via Fitbit" in tiny mono, "7:12 AM – 7:44 AM · 32m". A heart-rate line for the session span (raw samples bucketed to 30 seconds, min 10 samples, otherwise hidden) with avg and max labelled. A 2-column grid of NumberTiles for whichever of Distance, Calories, Steps, Avg HR, Max HR are present. Pace (min/km or min/mi) when both distance and duration exist and the type is running, walking or hiking.

### Heart rate detail sheet

`MetricKind.HEART_RATE`: hero is the day's average, status none, explanation "Your heart rate through the day. The line is 5-minute averages from your wearable." Week / Month / 6M bars of daily averages, MIN · AVG · MAX row. Under it a row "LOWEST TODAY 52 · HIGHEST 146" when `heartDay` is present. The estimated resting HR tag EST. is shown in the Resting heart rate sheet's hero when the selected day is estimated.

## Type, touch and icons

Type scale (`Type`):

| Style | Now | v0.5 |
|---|---|---|
| tiny | 9 sp | 11 sp |
| unit | 9 sp | 10 sp |
| label | 10 sp | 11 sp |
| status word | 9.5 sp | 11 sp |
| nav label | 8.5 sp | 10 sp |
| trio label | 9.5 sp | 11 sp |
| StatTile and ListRow secondary | 11 sp | 12 sp |
| small | 12 sp | 12 sp |
| body, tile, value, title, hero | unchanged | unchanged |

Letter-spacing stays 0.08 em on mono styles. The share card uses the same styles and must still fit 1080×1350.

Touch targets: menu, info and stepper circles stay 36–40 dp visually but get `Modifier.minimumInteractiveComponentSize()` (48 dp). Top-bar arrows become 44 dp square tap areas with a 16 dp chevron icon. The date text gets 12 dp vertical and 8 dp horizontal padding. Week strip cells are at least 48 dp tall. Segmented control options are at least 40 dp tall.

Icons replacing text glyphs:

- ▲ ▼ deltas: a 7 dp triangle drawn on a Canvas in good or bad colour.
- › row chevron, ▾ dropdown, ‹ › date arrows: `NavIcons.chevronRight`, `chevronDown`, `chevronLeft`.
- ✓ theme tick: `NavIcons.check`.
- ☾ ☀ on the span bar: `NavIcons.moonSmall` and a new `NavIcons.sun`.
- ✦ on ASK AI: `NavIcons.sparkle` at 12 dp before the label.

Sheet backgrounds: all three sheets use the theme's `bgMid`.

## Settings

- Goals: add **Active minutes** stepper (default 30, step 5, 10–120).
- Data: add **Read older data** (only when `READ_HEALTH_DATA_HISTORY` is declared and not granted).
- Units: weight follows the km / miles switch (kg / lb).

## Privacy

`docs/privacy.html` and the Play listing gain one sentence: with the "Read older data" permission fitdash reads up to 180 days of history instead of 30. Nothing else changes; still no internet permission.

## Out of scope

Contributors model and Movement index (v0.6), sleep debt, day timeline, action-first home, modules, widget, journal, weekly review, cycle, stress, cardio adaptability, nutrition macros, mindfulness sessions.

## Testing

Unit tests, pure Kotlin:

- `Workouts`: active minutes merges overlapping sessions; sessions under 2 minutes ignored; type and title naming; pace only for foot sports.
- `Heart`: 5-minute bucketing, gap breaking after 30 minutes, null under 10 samples, min/max/avg.
- `Sleep`: efficiency, awakenings ignore the leading and trailing awake, latency, null without stages, status bands.
- `Today`: Movement delta on today uses yesterday up to the same hour; past day uses the full previous day; distance/calories average excludes the selected day.
- `MetricSeries`: weekly averages for 6M, Monday-start weeks, correct count for 180 days.
- `Blood pressure`: second value carried through the generic series.
- `TodayViewModel`: resume within 2 minutes does not read; refresh always reads; selecting today after midnight shows today.

On device: every page in every theme at 1.0× and 1.3× font scale; workout sheet with and without heart rate; a day with three workouts; first-run grant screen lists the history permission; denying it leaves the 30-day behaviour; share card at the new type sizes.
