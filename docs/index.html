Features

Everything on four swipeable pages

Recovery, sleep, activity and trends, with the numbers that matter and none of the noise.

Recovery orb

One living orb with your recovery score, plus sleep, recovery and movement with change since yesterday.

Sleep

A score gauge, stages over the night, and your deep, light and REM against typical ranges.

Activity

Steps against your goal, workouts and naps, in plain cards.

Trends

Week and month views, so you can see where you are heading.

Five themes

Midnight Teal, Pastel, Techno, Graphite and Sunrise.

Day picker

Step back through any day from a week strip or a full calendar.

Ask AI

Share a summary of your day to any assistant you already use. Nothing leaves unless you send it.

Share your day

Turn today into a clean image in your current theme and send it anywhere.

Privacy

Private by design. Verifiable by anyone.

Your health data is read from Health Connect and processed on your phone. It only leaves when you choose to share it from the share sheet.

No internet permission

The release APK requests no INTERNET permission, so fitdash itself cannot connect to the internet. Data only leaves your phone when you choose to share it.

No servers or API

There is no backend. Data is read and processed on your phone.

No account or tracking

No sign-up, analytics, crash reporting, ads or trackers.

Writes only what you enter

fitdash writes only the water and height you enter yourself. It never changes or deletes any other data. Undo deletes only the water entry you just added.

Open source, GPL-3.0

Read every line. Any fork must stay open source too.

Built in the open

Release APKs are built and signed by GitHub Actions from the public source.

Verify it yourself

Check the APK you downloaded. The permissions list should show only Health Connect READ permissions, plus WRITE_HYDRATION and WRITE_HEIGHT, and no INTERNET.

aapt dump permissions fitdash.apk
apksigner verify --print-certs fitdash.apk
